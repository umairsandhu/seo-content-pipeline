Data & compliance

BYO keys (default, every tier): you pay DataForSEO directly — sub-cent per SERP call; the vendor never touches data revenue or liability. Managed data (Agency+): a thin, metered, transparent proxy at cost + margin — never a flat-fee pool. No central warehouse of Google/DataForSEO data; every workspace is isolated; every change is logged to its ledger.
